**Topic:** Trees
**Difficulty:** Easy

## Problem

Given the root of a binary tree, determine whether it is height-balanced: for every node, the heights of its left and right subtrees differ by at most 1.

## Technique Reminder

Binary tree traversal and properties (DFS/BFS based).

**Hint:** Most tree problems are recursive: solve for the root using solutions of its subtrees.

## Example

```
Input:  tree = [3,9,20,null,null,15,7]
Output: true
```
